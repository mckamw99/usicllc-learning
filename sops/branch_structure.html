Branch Structure

How USIC Academy branches are organized, and which branch to pick when you enroll people, build reports, or look up a learner.

Overview

01

In Docebo, a branch is a group of users that mirrors how USIC is organized. Every district is one branch, named for the district and coded with its 4-digit cost center number. People sit on their district's branch.

The one rule to remember: when you enroll people, build a report, or pick an audience, choose the district (or the region or division above it) and include its sub-branches.
4 levels
Company → Division → Region → District. Division and region appear only where the company uses them.
One district, one number
Each district carries its cost center number as its branch code. Central Indiana is also 2401. Search by either.
Access flows down
Your access covers the branches assigned to you and every branch below them, never above or beside.
Driven by the Learning Reference Table
District names and placement come from the SharePoint Learning Reference Table. LAT reviews it every quarter and updates Docebo to match.
Power Users do not create, rename, or move branches. That is an LAT task. To report a wrong name or a person in the wrong district, submit a Zendesk request.

The 4-Level Hierarchy

02

Click any level to see what it is and when to pick it.

1
Level 1 Company USIC, Reconn, Blood Hound, On Target

The top of each company’s tree. Everything for that company sits below it.

  • Picking a company reaches every employee in it, current and inactive.
  • Use only for company-wide required training, and only with LAT.
  • Career Technical Education (CTE) now sits under USIC. Advanced Gas Operations (AGO) sits under Reconn.
2
Level 2 Division e.g., East Division, West Division

Large groups of regions inside a company. Not every company has divisions.

  • Shown only where the Learning Reference Table has one.
  • South Division no longer exists. Its regions moved to East and West.
  • Pick a division only when you truly mean every region in it.
3
Level 3 Region e.g., South Atlantic, Southeast

Groups of districts inside a division or company.

  • Shown only where the Learning Reference Table has one.
  • Pick a region to reach all of its districts at once.
  • A district with no region sits right under the level above it.
4
Level 4 District e.g., Central Indiana · 2401

The branch people sit on. Its name comes from the Learning Reference Table and its branch code is the 4-digit cost center number.

  • The most common choice for enrolling and reporting.
  • Search by the district name or its 4-digit number.
  • People who leave stay here as inactive users, so their training history stays with the district.

Access & Scope

04

Each Power User is given a set of branches to manage, based on their cost center. Access always flows down: if you have a region, you see every district in it, but nothing above or beside it.

Missing someone you manage? They may sit in a district you were not given. Submit a Zendesk request instead of working around it.
Power User profileTypical branchesCan manage users in…
InstructorDistrictThe assigned districts. Can manage ILT sessions for those users.
Training Delivery Manager (TDM)District or RegionAll branches at or below the assigned level. Manages enrollments and learner records.

Enrolling by Branch

05

When you enroll by branch, the enrollment reaches everyone in that branch and every branch below it, as long as sub-branches are included.

Important: adding a course to a learning plan that is already published enrolls every current member of that plan right away. Test with a draft plan first.
