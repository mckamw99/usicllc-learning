Enrolling Users in E-Learning Courses

How Superadmins and Power Users enroll learners in single or multiple e-learning courses in Docebo

Overview

01

Users can be enrolled in courses by Superadmins or Power Users with granted permissions, or they can self-enroll. This guide describes how Superadmins and Power Users can enroll users in e-learning courses — individually or in bulk across multiple courses.

Single Course Enrollment

02
Vendor Resource: See the Docebo Help Article for additional reference.
  1. 1
    Navigate to the Courses PageIn the left sidebar, hover over the folder icon and select Content & Delivery › Courses to open the page displaying the list of courses.
  2. 2
    Select the CourseClick the title of the course to open the course configuration page.
  3. 3
    Enroll the User(s)In the top right corner, click the Manage enrollments button and select Enroll users.
  4. 4
    Set the Validity Dates (optional)Still on the Additional information tab, use the Start of validity and End of validity date pickers to limit when learners can access the course. Leave both blank for open-ended access. See Section 04 for how validity dates behave.
  5. 5
    Set the Enrollment AttributesThe Additional information tab displays the Enrollment attributes section. Configure the enrollment priority — Mandatory, Required, Recommended, or Optional. The enrollment priority is displayed on the course card in the learner's My courses and learning plans page.

Multiple Course Enrollment

03
Vendor Resource: See the Docebo Help Article for additional reference.
  1. 1
    Navigate to the Courses PageIn the left sidebar, hover over the folder icon and select Content & Delivery › Courses to open the page displaying the list of courses.
  2. 2
    Select the CoursesSelect the checkboxes next to each of the courses you want to enroll users in.
  3. 3
    Open the Enrollment ActionIn the bottom right corner, click the Choose action button and select Enroll users.
  4. 4
    Enroll User(s)In the right panel that opens, go through the steps — Users, Additional information, and Notifications — to enroll users in the selected courses. Click Next after each step to proceed.
  5. 5
    Set the Validity Dates (optional)Still on the Additional information tab, use the Start of validity and End of validity date pickers to limit when learners can access the course. Leave both blank for open-ended access. See Section 04 for how validity dates behave.
  6. 6
    Set the Enrollment AttributesThe Additional information tab displays the Enrollment attributes section. Configure the enrollment priority — Mandatory, Required, Recommended, or Optional.
Notice: A background job is started to process enrollments if the total number of enrollments exceeds 20. Remember that one enrollment is a combination of a single user and a single course — selecting 3 courses and 2 users creates 6 enrollments.

Enrollment Validity Dates

04

Validity dates control the window in which an enrolled learner can open and play a course. They are set per enrollment on the Additional information tab, and the same values apply to every learner included in that enrollment action.

Vendor Resource: See Managing enrollments of courses and sessions and Setting time validity for courses for additional reference.

How Validity Dates Behave

SettingEffect on the learner
Start of validity onlyThe course card appears in My courses and learning plans immediately, but the learner cannot launch content until the start date. No deadline is shown.
End of validity onlyAccess begins at enrollment. The end date is displayed on the course card as an expiration; once reached, the card remains visible but the course can no longer be opened.
Both datesAccess is limited to the window between the two dates. Outside the window the card is visible but locked.
Neither dateOpen-ended access. If the course itself has a validity period configured under Properties › Time options with Start calculation from the enrollment date, Docebo auto-populates the enrollment start/end from that setting when the status becomes Enrolled.

Editing Validity Dates on an Existing Enrollment

  1. 1
    Open the Enrollments TabFrom Content & Delivery › Courses, open the course and select the Enrollments tab.
  2. 2
    Edit the EnrollmentLocate the learner, click the ellipsis at the end of the row, and select Edit enrollment.
  3. 3
    Update the DatesChange Start of validity and/or End of validity, then save. To extend access for a learner whose enrollment has expired, push the end date forward — no re-enrollment is required.
Compliance courses: Do not use enrollment validity dates as a substitute for retraining cycles. Recurring requirements belong on the course under Properties › Certifications & Retraining so the renewal is tracked and reported. Validity dates only gate access; they do not create a certification record.
Learning plan enrollments: A validity period set at the learning-plan level cascades to every course in the plan and overrides course-level enrollment dates for that plan. If a course is in more than one plan, expiry in one plan does not remove access through the others.
Timezone (TBV in tenant): Docebo documents that end dates set through Enrollment Rules lock at 23:59:59 UTC. Whether the Enroll users panel applies the same UTC cutoff or the admin's platform timezone has not been verified on usic.training. If a hard cutoff time matters, set the end date one day later than the intended deadline until this is confirmed.

Re-Enrollments: Archive the Prior Record First

05

Docebo allows one active enrollment per learner per course. If a learner already has an enrollment — including a Completed one — the Enroll users panel will not create a second record and will not reset their progress. To require a learner to retake a course (annual refresher, failed audit, remediation, role change), archive the existing enrollment before enrolling them again.

Do not simply re-run Enroll users. A learner who is already enrolled is silently skipped, and a completed learner stays completed. Nothing changes on their record and no notification fires, so the retake never happens.

Which Path to Use

ScenarioPathNotes
Same course, retake now, no change to dates or levelArchive & re-enroll with Reset the tracking of the training materials checkedOne action. Learner is immediately re-enrolled with a fresh, blank record; prior completion is preserved in the archive.
Same course, but new validity dates, a different level, or a delayed startArchive & unenroll, then enroll via Section 02 or Section 03Two actions. Enrolling fresh exposes the full Additional information tab so validity dates and priority can be set. Recommended when the retake has a deadline.
Bulk retake across many learnersSelect all learners on the Enrollments tab › Choose action › Archive enrollmentSame options pop-up applies to the whole selection. Disable enrollment/completion notifications first if the group is large. For very large sets use the LAT script toolkit instead of the UI.
Recurring compliance cycleConfigure Properties › Certifications & Retraining on the coursePreferred over manual archiving for anything on a fixed cadence — the platform tracks expiry and renewal, and the archive step becomes unnecessary.

Step-by-Step: Archive Then Re-Enroll

  1. 1
    Confirm the Existing EnrollmentOpen the course, go to the Enrollments tab, and locate the learner. Note their current status (Enrolled, In progress, Completed) and completion date — you will need this if the retake is questioned later.
  2. 2
    Archive the EnrollmentClick the ellipsis at the end of the row and select Archive enrollment. Choose Archive & re-enroll (retake now) or Archive & unenroll (retake later or with new dates). Check Reset the tracking of the training materials unless the learner should resume where they left off.
  3. 3
    Enroll Again (unenroll path only)If you chose Archive & unenroll, follow the steps in Section 02 or Section 03, setting validity dates and enrollment priority as needed.
  4. 4
    VerifySwitch the Enrollment type filter to Archived enrollments to confirm the prior record is present, then back to Active enrollments to confirm the new record shows status Enrolled with 0% progress.
Archiving is permanent. Archived enrollments cannot be restored to active. Completion history is preserved for reporting, but if you may need to reactivate the original record, unenroll instead. See the full Archiving Enrollment for Learning Plans and Courses guide for cascade behavior on learning plans and the Options Reference.
Validity dates on Archive & re-enroll (TBV in tenant): Docebo documentation does not state whether the re-enroll pop-up carries forward the original validity dates, clears them, or lets you set new ones. Until verified on usic.training, use the Archive & unenroll path whenever the retake needs a specific access window.
