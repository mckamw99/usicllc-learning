01

Run the four universal checks on every USIC Academy ticket before opening a How-To. Most tickets close at one of these checks, and none of them needs an escalation.

Triage flow

Universal checks (in order)

  1. 1
    Platform statusGo to status.docebo.com and find Enterprise Plan Platforms – US (Virginia).
    • Not Operational: tell the user there is a platform outage, a communication will go out once it is fixed, and to try again then. Close or pend the ticket. Do not escalate.
  2. 2
    The Spot“Can you log into The Spot, or any other app that uses your normal company login?”
    • No: the problem is The Spot, not USIC Academy. Handle it as a The Spot / SSO issue. Do not escalate to the Learning Team.
  3. 3
    Employment statusLook the user up in Workday by EEID.
    • Not found or inactive: refer the user to HR or Workday support. Do not escalate to the Learning Team.
  4. 4
    Connection & deviceConfirm other apps or websites load. On a hotspot, check the source phone has a strong signal. On a company field device, confirm it is logged into the SOTI Hub.
    • Weak or no connection: have the user move to better Wi-Fi or signal and retry.

AHow-To: Can’t Log In

02

Most login tickets are a platform outage, The Spot being down, an inactive Workday record, or a stale app session.

Before you start: universal checks 1–4 passed (platform up, The Spot works, user active in Workday, connection good).
  1. 1
    Ask how they are logging in“Are you using the phone app, a tablet, or a computer?”Phone app → step 2. Tablet or computer → step 3.
  2. 2
    Phone (USIC Academy app)
    • Force-quit the app and reopen it.
    • Confirm other apps or websites work on the same connection; if not, move to better Wi-Fi or signal.
    • On a company field device, confirm it is logged into the SOTI Hub and the app is up to date (IT pushes app updates).
    • Still failing → step 4.
  3. 3
    Tablet or computer (browser)
    • Confirm other websites load. On a hotspot, check the source phone’s signal.
    • Send the user to usic.docebosaas.com and have them sign in with their normal The Spot credentials.
    • Can log into The Spot elsewhere but not here → step 4.
  4. 4
    EscalateUse the universal fields (Escalation tab) plus confirmation that The Spot works elsewhere.
Resolves at Tier 1: platform outage, The Spot down, inactive in Workday, bad connection, stale app session (force-quit fixes it).

BHow-To: Course Won’t Load or User Is Stuck

03

Most “stuck” courses have a missed interaction: an unwatched video, an unticked box, or a hotspot nobody tapped.

Before you start: universal checks 1–4 passed (platform up, The Spot works, user active in Workday, connection good).
  1. 1
    Identify the issue type“Are you stuck inside the course and can’t finish it, or does the course show incomplete after you already finished it?”Already finished, shows incomplete → How-To D. Stuck or can’t open it → continue.
  2. 2
    Does the course load?“Does the course open and load at all?”
    • Yes: go to step 3.
    • No (black or blank screen, spinning, “no training material”): with platform status clear and a good connection confirmed, escalate with the universal fields plus the exact course name.
  3. 3
    In-course interaction checksHave the user try each of these while you stay on the call:
    • Scroll to the top of the current section — any checkboxes, prompts or videos missed? Click them again.
    • Tap every logical area, not only text and buttons: icons, arrows, images. On a picture of equipment or a device, tap the equipment itself — it may hold a hidden prompt.
    • Open the three-line (hamburger) menu, if there is one, and note the section title they’re stuck in. No menu: note that.
    • If there is a menu: can they skip ahead to another section? Are they stuck there too?
    • On a company field device: confirm it is logged into the SOTI Hub.
  4. 4
    Still stuck? EscalateUniversal fields plus course name, section name, confirmation all checks were tried, and a screenshot of where they are stuck.
Resolves at Tier 1: a missed interaction (unwatched video, unticked box, untapped hotspot), SOTI Hub logged out, poor connection.

CHow-To: 403 Forbidden / “You Do Not Have Access”

04

A 403 almost always means the user opened a link that wasn’t meant for them. The fix is to find the user’s own copy of the course, not to grant access to the link.

Usual causes: a direct link built for another user, a link forwarded from someone in a different district or role, or a different course version assigned by role or region.

Before you start: universal checks 1–4 passed (platform up, The Spot works, user active in Workday, connection good).
  1. 1
    Identify the course“What’s the name of the course or learning plan you were trying to open?”
    • Don’t know: ask where the link came from and who sent it. The name is usually in that email or message; an automatic USIC Academy assignment email includes it.
    • Need to see the link: have the user copy it, not tap it (tapping on Android opens it and triggers the 403 again).
      • Desktop: right-click → Copy Link → paste into Teams or email.
      • Outlook app (Android): press and hold the link → Copy link → paste into Teams or email.
      • Android Mail app: press and hold the link → Copy URL / Copy address → send by chat or email.
  2. 2
    Find the user’s own copy
    • Mobile app: check the homepage sections Assigned Courses – Not Started and Assigned Courses – In Progress. Not there: user menu (top left) → My Courses and Learning Plans → search by course name.
    • Browser: sign in at usic.docebosaas.com with The Spot credentials, then check the same homepage sections or search My Courses and Learning Plans.
    • Found: have the user open it from there. Close the ticket.
  3. 3
    Not found? Send to the Manager, L&DThe user’s Manager, Learning & Development confirms whether the course is assigned and assigns the correct version if needed. Find the manager for the user’s district in the Learning Reference Table on the LAT SharePoint site.
  4. 4
    Escalate only if all are trueCourse name confirmed; user searched the app and the browser; the manager confirmed the right version is assigned; the 403 still appears. Include the universal fields plus the exact course name, the link, and the manager’s name.
Resolves at Tier 1: forwarded or someone else’s link, course found under My Courses and Learning Plans, assignment fixed by the Manager, Learning & Development.

DHow-To: Finished but Not Marked Complete

05

A course only shows Completed when every required item inside it is complete. The usual cause is a failed test, a skipped item, or a course closed before its progress saved.

New How-To — pending Learning Team review. No helpdesk article covered this issue before; these steps fill that gap.
Before you start: universal checks 1–4 passed (platform up, The Spot works, user active in Workday, connection good).
  1. 1
    Confirm what the user seesHave them open My Courses and Learning Plans, search the course, and read its status (Not started, In progress, Completed) and any score.
    • Shows Completed: the record is fine. If they’re looking at a learning plan, check whether another course in the plan is still open.
  2. 2
    Check for an unfinished requirementAsk whether the course had a test or quiz and what score they got, and whether every section, video or interaction was finished (see How-To B, step 3). A failed test or a skipped item leaves the course In progress.
  3. 3
    Relaunch and exit properlyHave the user reopen the course on a good connection, go to the last page or section, and leave using the course’s own Exit / Close button — not by closing the app or browser tab. Then refresh My Courses and Learning Plans and recheck the status.
  4. 4
    Still incomplete? EscalateUniversal fields plus the exact course name, the date they finished, what the course shows now, and a screenshot of the status.
Resolves at Tier 1: learner looking at the wrong item, unfinished test or interaction, course closed before progress saved.
Learning Team reference: the admin-side fixes for stuck completions are in the Course Stuck “In Progress” FAQ.

SOP: Escalation to the Learning Team

06

Escalate only when all four universal checks pass and every step of the matching How-To has been tried and noted in the ticket.

Why detail matters. The Learning Team sees only that a course was attempted, not where the user got stuck. A ticket without the details below comes back to Tier 1.

Do not escalate when

  • status.docebo.com shows an outage
  • The Spot is down, or the user cannot log into The Spot anywhere
  • The user is inactive or missing in Workday
  • The user’s connection is poor and they haven’t retried on a better one
  • (403 tickets) the user’s Manager, Learning & Development has not yet confirmed the assignment

Every escalation includes

  • Full name and Employee ID (EEID)
  • Device (iPhone, Android phone, tablet, PC) and access method (USIC Academy app or browser — which browser)
  • App version, if known
  • Date and time of the last attempt
  • Short description of what happens (login loop, error text, blank screen, where they are stuck)
  • Screenshot(s) of the problem
  • The steps already completed, by How-To step number

Add for each issue type

Purpose & Scope

08

Tier 1 Helpdesk resolves most USIC Academy tickets by running four standard checks and one issue-specific How-To. A ticket goes to the Learning Team only when every step has been done and documented.

This guide combines the three USIC Academy helpdesk articles (Login, Unable to Complete a Course, 403 Forbidden) into one SOP with a matching How-To for each common issue. It is for Tier 1 Helpdesk agents taking USIC Academy tickets through Zendesk.
