About the dashboard

01
Q

What is this dashboard?

It shows how new techs do in their first two years. It shows the good things they do, like closing locates. It also shows the costs, like damages and people who leave.

Q

Who is it for?

It is for leaders and trainers. You can use it to see which training helps new techs the most.

Q

Where do I find it?

Open it in Power BI with this link. Start on the first page. It tells you how to use it.

Q

How new is the data?

The data gets new numbers one time each day.

Q

How do I pick what I see?

Use the boxes at the top of the page. They are called filters. Click a name to pick it. Click it again to take it off.

Q

What filters are there?

You can pick a division, region or district. You can pick a training class. You can also pick the hire type, like a brand new hire or someone who came back.

Q

Why did my Hire Type pick follow me to the next page?

That filter stays the same on all the pages. This way you do not have to pick it again. The Compare page is the one that does not follow. It has its own filters for A and B.

Q

What happens when I put my mouse on a bar?

A small box pops up with more facts. On the Inputs page, it shows LPH and TPH.

Q

How do I clear all my picks?

Click the eraser in the top corner of a filter. You can also use “Reset to default” at the top of Power BI.

The numbers

03
Q

What is Modified Revenue?

It is the money techs make, minus the money we lose. Here is how it works:

  • We add money for each locate a tech closes.
  • We take away money when a tech leaves the company.
  • We take away money when a tech gets hurt at work.
  • We take away money for some truck crashes.
  • We take away money for damages that were the tech’s fault.
Q

Why is it “per hour”?

Some groups work more hours than others. Per hour makes it fair. A small group and a big group can be compared the same way.

Q

What does tenure mean?

Tenure is how many months a tech has been on the job. We start counting on the day they were hired or the day their training class started. We use whichever day is later.

Q

Why does one tech show up in many bars?

A tech’s work goes in the month they were in when they did it. A tech in month 5 adds to the month 5 bar. Later, they add to the month 6 bar too.

Q

What are LPH and TPH?

LPH means locates per hour. TPH means tickets per hour. They show how fast a tech gets work done.

Q

What does HP ILT mean?

HP ILT means the tech finished a High Profile class with a teacher in USIC Academy.

Q

What does Nested mean?

Nested means the new tech spent time working next to a skilled tech before working alone.

Things that might look odd

04
Q

Why are the first bars below zero?

In the first months, new techs are still learning. They do not close many locates yet. Some new techs also leave. Leaving costs a lot, so the bars go below zero. This is normal.

Q

Does a bar below zero mean the tech is bad?

No. It means they are new. Most techs get better as the months go on.

Q

Why is the last bar (13–24 months) different?

Most bars are 2 months long. The last bar is 12 months long. Be careful when you compare it to the others.

Q

Why is a chart blank?

Your filters may be too tight. There may be no techs that match all your picks. Take off one filter at a time until you see data.

Q

Why did a number change from yesterday?

New data comes in each day. Old records can get fixed too. So numbers can move a little.

Q

Why does a tech say “No class form”?

That tech does not have a training class form on file. They still count in the totals.

Q

Is this the official record?

No. It is a tool to help you see trends. For official numbers, ask the team that owns that data.
